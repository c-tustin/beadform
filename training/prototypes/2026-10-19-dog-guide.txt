Floppy-eared dog prototype
DIGITAL PROTOTYPE — NOT PHYSICALLY SAMPLED
149 size 11/0 seed beads; 0.25 mm thread; 1 working length(s).
Starting, knots, tails and finishing need additional hole space; check your actual beads.

1. make body
Start the project with a tail for finishing. String five foundation beads and circle through the first. Work between the previous round’s up-beads: add one normally, two at each marked increase; at a decrease add one then pass through the two listed up-beads. Step up through the first new bead at each round end. Draw the final five up-beads together. Optional filling: pause before the first decrease and lightly fill the hollow form with clean soft plastic film or craft filling. Keep the rim and bead holes clear; then decrease and close. The chart shows the whole surface; use the exact sequence for shaping and step-ups. This shaped peyote form is a draft: check the cup shape and tension with your beads before continuing. Do not cut or finish the working line here; continue to the next step.
