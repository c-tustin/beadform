# reviewed pattern references — 2026-09-20

This is a curated source review, not a scraped training corpus. No source photographs, charts or full texts have been bundled. No third-party project has been turned into a generator template. Rights descriptions below are the source's stated terms, not a worldwide legal clearance.

1. Thérèse de Dillmont, Encyclopedia of Needlework. Project Gutenberg ebook 20776 identifies the work as public domain in the USA. Credits include Susan Skinner, Julie Barkley, the Online Distributed Proofreading Team and Richard Tonsing. https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/20776
   Reviewed text: https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/20776/pg20776-images.html
   Relevant content: Turkish stitch with beads (figure 384); before/intermediate/finished-state teaching in figures 534–536. These are historical knitting and knotting techniques, not verified CRAW/peyote animal recipes. Transfer to beadform: a backward/forward pair of replay controls makes the before/after state of each actual computed move accessible. This is an original UI implementation, not an image reproduction.

2. Barbour's New Bead Book, National Trading Company, 1924. Scan donated by Mary Lou Davis and hosted by Antique Pattern Library. https://www.antiquepatternlibrary.org/pub/PDF/BarbourNewBead.pdf
   Page 1 describes the original as public domain to the library's knowledge, but licenses the photo-edited scan under CC BY-NC-SA 2.5: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/2.5/
   Includes loom work and bead patterns. Link only; excluded from copying/model context/training. A historical original and a modern edited scan must not be treated as having identical terms.

3. Melinda Barta, Beadwork: Peyote Stitch—Basics and Beyond, Interweave 2010.
   https://www.interweave.com/wp-content/uploads/peyote-basics-beyond-barta-illustrations.pdf
   Explicit copyright/all-rights-reserved notice. Useful free-to-read technique reference, clearly labelled link-only. No source charts/text imported into app, model context or training.
